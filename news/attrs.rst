**Added:**

* ``WDFReader`` now extracts ``ExposureTime`` (CCD exposure in seconds) and
  ``LaserPower`` (ND-filter transmission in percent) from the ``WXDM`` and
  ``WXIS`` blocks and stores them as ``DataArray`` attributes.  Works for
  single spectra, 2-D maps, and 3-D volume scans.
